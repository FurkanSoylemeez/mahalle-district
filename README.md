# MAHALLE (DISTRICT)

A 2D top-down narrative-driven neighborhood game developed as a solo project in Godot.

## Overview

MAHALLE (DISTRICT) is a single-player game focused on neighborhood life, social identity, player choice, and interconnected gameplay systems.

The project combines:

- Gameplay systems
- Narrative design
- Quest design
- NPC interactions
- Player progression
- Economy and work systems
- Social Reputation
- Street Reputation

## Core Systems

### Social Reputation

Represents how the neighborhood perceives Jacob as a reliable, helpful, and community-oriented person.

### Street Reputation

Represents Jacob's presence and credibility in the street environment, including toughness, intimidation, and his ability to gain support during conflicts.

The two systems are designed as distinct forms of social progression rather than a simple good/evil morality system.

## Gameplay Structure

The game is structured around a 30-day progression.

The four main weeks each focus on a different surrounding neighborhood:

- **Week 1 — West Neighborhood**
- **Week 2 — North Neighborhood**
- **Week 3 — South Neighborhood / Port**
- **Week 4 — East Neighborhood**

Each neighborhood has its own identity, local problems, NPC relationships, and gameplay context.

The final two days bring together the systems, relationships, and information established throughout the previous four weeks.

The project is currently in active development.

## Design Focus

The main design goal is to connect narrative and gameplay systems so that player choices have meaningful consequences across the neighborhood.

Rather than treating story, quests, progression, and social systems as separate layers, MAHALLE (DISTRICT) is designed around their interaction.
